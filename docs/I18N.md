# Languages (i18n)

Owner decision 2026-11-05: the site is available in English (source),
Russian, Spanish, Chinese (simplified) and Hindi.

## How it works

- The visitor's language comes from the `docroute-lang` cookie, otherwise
  from the browser's languages, otherwise English. Addresses do not change.
  The switcher is in the header and the footer (`LanguageSwitcher`).
- Components are written in English as before. The site's JSX runtime
  (`tsconfig` → `"jsxImportSource": "@/i18n"`, `src/i18n/jsx-runtime.ts`)
  translates every piece of text a component renders, plus `placeholder`,
  `title`, `alt` and `aria-label`, using the catalog of the visitor's
  language. Nothing has to be wrapped in `t()`.
- Catalogs: `src/i18n/catalogs/{ru,es,zh,hi}.json`, keyed by the English
  text with whitespace collapsed. Variables are `{0}`, `{1}`…, inline
  elements `<0/>`, `<1/>`…; a translation may reorder them but must keep
  all of them (tested). English dates ("15 March 2027") are reformatted for
  the language automatically.
- Text without a translation stays in English, so a missing entry never
  breaks a page. New or changed English text simply shows in English until
  it is translated.
- Text that must never change: `translate="no"` on the element (the logo
  does this). The word typed to delete an account (`DELETE`) is kept out of
  the catalogs on purpose.
- Strings outside JSX (alerts, `document.title`): `t()` / `useT()` from
  `@/i18n/client`, or `getT()` from `@/i18n/server` in server code.

## Updating translations

1. `npm run i18n:extract -- /tmp/keys.json` lists every English string the
   site can show, with the files it comes from.
2. Add the missing keys with their translations to the four catalogs.
3. `npm test` checks that every translation keeps the variables and
   elements of its English text.

Legal pages and verified route steps are translated for convenience; the
English text and the official sources linked on every step prevail.
