# "Your passport decides your route" (en, ru, hi)

Built from what worked on the EN TikTok account (2026-11-10): "Built for your
passport" 898 views, "3 days left" 843 (bright coral open), film 843; weakest
were plain UI videos (button 571, universities 647). So: a bright coral open
with a passport flag slamming in every 0.45 s → same university in Slovakia,
three passports, three routes (entry visa needed / visa-free entry but a
residence permit to study / EU: register on arrival; visa lists per EU
Regulation 2018/1806) → most guides give everyone the same list → DocRoute
builds the route for your passport, official source for every rule → CTA
"Comment your passport" + "Link in profile" (comments lift reach).
en/hi: India, Brazil, EU; ru: Kazakhstan, Georgia, EU.

```bash
python3 marketing/passport/voice.py <kokoro models> en ru hi
node marketing/passport/render.mjs en ru hi
```
